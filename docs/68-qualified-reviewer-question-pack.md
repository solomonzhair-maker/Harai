- End-to-end funds-flow diagrams for provider-managed funds and direct integration options, including every account, balance, settlement obligation and failure/unknown-outcome point.

Do not fill unknowns with assumptions. Each answer should cite the document, clause, source version/date, factual assumptions, scope/applicability, reviewer name/qualification, conclusion, open conditions and review date. If the evidence is insufficient, record **UNRESOLVED** and identify exactly what is needed.

## 2. Ugandan payments counsel — classification, licensing and consumer protection

1. Based on the exact planned legal entity, customer/merchant contracts, services and funds flow, which statutory definitions and licence categories under the National Payment Systems Act and regulations are engaged, if any? Assess aggregator/integrator/gateway activity and whether the intended platform receives or controls funds, acquires transactions, initiates transfers, settles merchants, or only supplies technology.
2. Does the entity need a PSP, PSO, payment-instrument or other licence/approval before offering each proposed activity? Which exemptions, if any, are textually available and supported by facts? What written evidence or regulator determination is required before launch?
3. How do the August 2025 BoU NPS Oversight Policy Framework, all relevant regulations/amendments, circulars and licence conditions apply to the intended model? Identify any amendments not reflected in ULII's consolidated view and provide the current authoritative text.
4. Does the NPS (Consumer Protection) Regulations, 2022 apply to the entity? If so, identify the exact obligations for policy governance, consumer information, fairness, complaints/redress, complaint records and reporting. Confirm the regulation 6 monthly complaint report, its trigger, format/channel, submission evidence and any subsequent BoU direction.
5. Which current BoU Consumer Protection Guidelines apply, to which institution categories, and do they prescribe a specific complaint acknowledgement, response or resolution SLA? Provide exact clauses and effective/version date; do not infer an SLA from a landing page.
6. What approval is needed for outsourcing or using cloud/third-party technical operations under the current law? Which arrangement is a regulated outsourcing/core operation versus an ordinary technology supplier? How should contractual accountability and BoU access/oversight be evidenced?
7. What approvals/limitations apply to cross-border payment services, currencies, merchant acquisition, product partnerships and third-party integrations? What launch scope must remain disabled until approvals exist?
8. Which applicable contract, consumer, electronic transactions, AML/CFT, recordkeeping, limitation-period and evidence rules affect the complaint workflow? Separate statutory limitation periods from operational complaint SLAs and provider notification windows.
9. Which records and customer-facing disclosures are mandatory for complaints, fees, refunds, reversals, finality, settlement delays, unauthorised transactions and escalation? Which communications must be approved before use?
10. Provide a written role-and-obligation memo mapping each conclusion to facts, legislation/regulation clause, applicable entity/service, required evidence, owner and unresolved condition. State explicitly where no conclusion is possible yet.

## 3. Privacy counsel / Data Protection Officer

1. Which HARAI entities/roles are data collectors, controllers and processors for each flow? Which providers or cloud vendors are independent controllers, processors or subprocessors, and what contracts/instructions are required?
2. Must the entity register with PDPO, in which category, and what certificate/current status proves registration? What annual compliance report obligations apply under regulation 50 and the current PDPO notice?
3. Does the PDPO Knowledge Center notice updated 25 August 2026, which lists 23 November 2026, apply to the entity and current reporting period? Confirm the correct template, filing channel, contents, accountable signatory and evidence of submission. Do not submit until authorized.
4. What is the legal effect and scope of regulation 50's annual report requirement and PDPO's stated expansion of scope? Which complaints and breach details are required, and what minimization/redaction should apply?
5. What privacy notice, lawful basis, purpose limitation, data-subject rights and collection-minimization duties apply to transaction evidence, identity/KYC documents, complaint narratives, provider payloads, fraud indicators, communications and exports?
6. What retention periods or retention triggers apply by record class under DPPA, NPS rules (if applicable), provider contracts, tax/accounting duties, litigation holds and limitation rules? Identify the exact legal source for each duration; do not use a single global period.
7. Which cross-border storage/processing restrictions, registrations, transfer conditions or processor contractual clauses apply to selected hosting and provider routes?
8. What are the breach assessment, immediate PDPO notification, prescribed channel/form and data-subject notification requirements? Define who decides, what evidence is preserved, and how quickly privacy/legal triage must occur without inventing a numeric statutory timer.
9. What DPIA or risk assessment is required for the actual processing, and which safeguards are mandatory for privileged access, evidence attachments, exports, deletion, backups and support tooling?
10. Provide a written privacy applicability and retention schedule with clause citations, named accountable owners, escalation process, reporting-calendar entries, approved notices and unresolved items.

3. For each candidate funds flow, who controls the specified service before transfer, who owes the merchant/customer, who holds funds, who is principal/agent, and when is HARAI's fee earned? Document IFRS 15 analysis based on contracts and actual promises, not on branding or technical routing.
4. What are the accounting and reconciliation treatments for provider-held funds, settlement receivables/payables, reserves, fees, refunds, reversals, disputes, unknown transaction outcomes, provider outages and settlement breaks?
5. Under what facts would IAS 37 or other applicable requirements require recognition/disclosure of provisions or contingent liabilities for complaints, fraud, refunds, claims or litigation? Which evidence supports probability, measurement and reassessment?
6. What chart-of-accounts mapping, subledger boundaries, daily/period-end reconciliation, maker-checker controls, write-off authority and close procedures are needed? Who owns unresolved breaks and what evidence is required before correction?
7. What financial records must be retained and for how long, and how do those obligations interact with DPPA minimization/deletion and legal holds?
8. Provide hypothetical worked examples for each candidate flow, including assumptions, journal entries, reconciliation entries, fee presentation, treatment of customer/merchant money, exceptions and financial-statement disclosures. Mark each example conditional until contract/funds-flow facts are confirmed.
9. What external audit evidence and control narratives will be expected if HARAI is a covered PSP/PSO? What system logs, immutable events, approvals and reports must be produced?

## 6. Cross-disciplinary deadline and evidence registry acceptance criteria

Every candidate deadline must identify: source/version and exact clause; jurisdiction; covered entity and role; product/channel; triggering event; calendar/business-day and time-zone semantics; start/end rules; pause/resume conditions; holiday handling if relevant; required action/content; accountable owner and backup; destination and authorized sender; delivery/acknowledgement evidence; escalation route; effective date; supersession history; and reviewer approval.

Keep these categories distinct:
- BoU monthly consumer-complaint reporting for covered licensees (NPS Consumer Protection Regulations, 2022, reg. 6; 15 days after month end).
- PDPO annual data-protection compliance reporting for covered registered organisations (regulation 50 guidance describes 90 days after financial-year end; current PDPO notice lists 23 November 2026; verify applicability and current filing instructions).
- Privacy breach reporting to PDPO (the Act uses “immediately” under the relevant conditions; qualified privacy/legal reviewer must define operational escalation and prescribed process).
- Provider-specific transaction error/reversal windows and provider response/resolution SLAs (UNVERIFIED until contract/product/account evidence supports them).
- Internal service targets and statutory limitation periods (not interchangeable).

## 1. Guardrails and materials to provide

This pack is not an instruction to contact any external party. Provider follow-up and regulator contact are not authorized by this document. The user remains the accountable internal reviewer; external legal, privacy and accounting conclusions must be made by appropriately qualified people who can inspect the actual facts and documents.

Before a reviewer concludes, provide as applicable:
- Current legal-entity details, ownership/management, business plan and exact proposed service catalogue.
- End-to-end funds-flow diagrams for provider-managed funds and direct integration options, including every account, balance, settlement obligation and failure/unknown-outcome point.
- Customer, merchant and provider contract drafts; provider account/product roles and any attributable written provider responses.
- System/data-flow diagram, hosting region, subprocessors, data inventory, access model, security/incident procedures and draft privacy notices.
- DOC-31 requirements baseline, DOC-28 funds-flow comparison, DOC-42 ledger/reconciliation proposal, DOC-43 threat model, DOC-64 complaint-case proposal, DOC-65 review matrix, and DOC-67 source/obligation matrix.

Do not fill unknowns with assumptions. Each answer should cite the document, clause, source version/date, factual assumptions, scope/applicability, reviewer name/qualification, conclusion, open conditions and review date. If the evidence is insufficient, record **UNRESOLVED** and identify exactly what is needed.

10. Provide a written role-and-obligation memo mapping each conclusion to facts, legislation/regulation clause, applicable entity/service, required evidence, owner and unresolved condition. State explicitly where no conclusion is possible yet.

## 6. Cross-disciplinary deadline and evidence registry acceptance criteria

Every candidate deadline must identify: source/version and exact clause; jurisdiction; covered entity and role; product/channel; triggering event; calendar/business-day and time-zone semantics; start/end rules; pause/resume conditions; holiday handling if relevant; required action/content; accountable owner and backup; destination and authorized sender; delivery/acknowledgement evidence; escalation route; effective date; supersession history; and reviewer approval.

Keep these categories distinct:
- BoU monthly consumer-complaint reporting for covered licensees (NPS Consumer Protection Regulations, 2022, reg. 6; 15 days after month end).
- PDPO annual data-protection compliance reporting for covered registered organisations (regulation 50 guidance describes 90 days after financial-year end; current PDPO notice lists 23 November 2026; verify applicability and current filing instructions).
- Privacy breach reporting to PDPO (the Act uses “immediately” under the relevant conditions; qualified privacy/legal reviewer must define operational escalation and prescribed process).
- Provider-specific transaction error/reversal windows and provider response/resolution SLAs (UNVERIFIED until contract/product/account evidence supports them).
- Internal service targets and statutory limitation periods (not interchangeable).

## 1. How to read this crosswalk

- **Normative criterion:** The exact acceptance criteria remain in DOC-31 at the referenced section and requirement ID; this table deliberately does not paraphrase or replace them.
- **Design/source reference:** A candidate document reference based on the existing DOC-34 dependency/evidence entries and the current architecture cross-references. A document reference is not proof that its content fully satisfies the requirement. Where a section-level source could not be confirmed, it is marked `MISSING` or the section is explicitly not confirmed.
- **Evidence state:** `NOT CREATED` means no linked, attributable execution/design-review evidence is recorded here. `BLOCKED` means a material provider, legal/compliance, finance/accounting, owner decision or other gate dependency is unresolved. A row may have both a missing artifact and a blocking dependency.
- **Owner/reviewer:** Role labels are proposals only. No named owner, specialist or independent reviewer is appointed by this file. The accountable requirements reviewer is the user; ChatGPT may support internal documentation review but is not independent third-party sign-off.
- **Gate:** Use DOC-46 and DOC-62 for the full gate criteria. Mapping or planned test IDs never close G1/G2.
